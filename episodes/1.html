Episode 1: Samuel Baidoo

The first episode! We talk with artist, theater maker and game designer Samuel Baidoo about Plum Road Tea Dream.

How do we mourn for people we don't know?

During this episode, we explore how virtual spaces in games can become places for rituals.

We had the pleasure of talking with Samuel Baidoo about their projects, their understanding of games as a medium, and the relationship between game making and performance.

Through Plum Road Tea Dream, we encounter themes of grief, acceptance, community, and intimacy, exploring how interactive experiences can invite both individuals and communities to confront loss and find new ways of processing it.

We also discuss the game's visual language, its blend of magical and technological elements, and some of its most iconic design choices.

This episode is about slowing down, while also being witnesses, and discovering how virtual worlds, and games, can become places of collective healing.
