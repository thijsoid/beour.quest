About BE OUR QUEST

BE OUR QUEST is a monthly radio show broadcast live from Radio WORM in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Each episode, Alessia and Thijs invite a special guest to talk about games, experimentation and community. We feel so much energy around us in the local experimental games scene, and with this radio show, we hope to document and archive some of that!

Alessia Vadacca (she / her) is a poet and designer who loves chaos. Check her out on Instagram

Thijs van Loenhout (he / they) is a game maker and artist who doesn't love chaos. Check them out on itch.io, Instagram

Like the show, have feedback, or want to get in touch? Reach out to us via the above channels or approach us at WORM!
